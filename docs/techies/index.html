Meet techies who work near you

Shy to start a chat with a stranger at the tech park? Start here. These are free Telegram groups for people who work in the same area: share referrals, job leads, interview tips and tech questions, and maybe meet for chai.

Manyata Techies Manyata Tech Park, Hebbal, Nagawara · coming soon Marathahalli / Brookefield Techies Marathahalli, Brookefield, Kundalahalli, ITPL · coming soon

You need the Telegram app. Your phone number stays hidden from other members if you set Privacy > Phone Number to "Nobody".

Good in the groups

  • Referrals: "My team is hiring a backend dev, DM me."
  • Job leads, interview tips and salary bands.
  • Tech questions and free resources that helped you.
  • "Anyone up for lunch near gate 5 on Friday?"

Not allowed (you will be removed)

  • Asking anyone for money for a job, training or "processing".
  • Spam, ads, crypto, betting or MLM.
  • Flirting, unwanted private messages, or anything that makes someone feel unsafe.
  • Sharing another person's phone number, photo or personal details.
  • Anyone under 18.

A real job never asks you to pay. If someone asks for a fee, a deposit or your bank details, do not pay, and report them.

Report a problem

In the group, reply to the message and tag @admin. Or write to support@nxtduo.com with the group name and what happened. We act within 24 hours.

PUBLIK does not see your private chats. Telegram's own terms and privacy policy apply inside the groups.
